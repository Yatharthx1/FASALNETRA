What we learned from ICAR-IISR

We consulted Dr. Savita Kolhe, ICAR-IISR Indore. Three points shaped the design.

  • Warn about 15 days early. After symptoms show, it is too late. So we predict first.
  • Drone imaging is unreliable: rotor wind shakes the plants. So the drone only sprays; the farmer's phone collects the evidence.
  • Farmers trust the weekly ICAR advisory. So every spray recommendation comes from it, with the source read out.

Proof 2 · Sentinel-2 as a scout, not a judge

Each 10 m cell is compared with its own field and its own history.

  • Demo field, scene 1 Aug 2025: 27 of 69 cells flagged, one compact patch of 0.67 acre.
  • Cells on the field edge are never flagged: they mix in the neighbour's land.
  • Monsoon clouds left only 4 clear scenes in Jul to Sep 2025. That is why the farmer's walk confirms what the satellite suggests.

Trust · every signal votes

No single signal decides. Doubt sends a human.

  • Photos + weather + satellite only: Medium 66% → Drone Didi checks first
  • Add the farmer's "yes, whiteflies" and trap counts: High 77% → zone-only spray
  • Patch in a low spot: spray blocked, waterlogging checked first
  • Always "shak" (suspected), never a certain diagnosis
